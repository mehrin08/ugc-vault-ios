Common questions

Do I need an account? No. UGC Vault works without sign-up.

Where is my data stored? On your device. We don't upload, sell or share it. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data? Remove items in the app, or delete the app from your iPhone.

Can I get it on iPad or Android? Not yet. Email us if you'd like it.
