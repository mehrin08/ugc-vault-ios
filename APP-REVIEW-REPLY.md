2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
UGC Vault is a productivity app for UGC (user-generated content) creators, meaning freelancers who make short videos for brands. These creators juggle many brand deals at once, and their work is scattered across notes, spreadsheets and DMs. UGC Vault keeps it in one place: daily and content-batching task lists per brand, scripts/hooks/captions, brand deals and payment tracking with a monthly earnings goal, a calendar of deadlines, and reminder notifications. The value is that creators stop missing deadlines and payments and can see their whole business at a glance.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
Sign in with the demo account in the App Review Information section. It already contains sample data. Anyone can also create a new account on the sign-in screen with an email and password.
- Tasks tab: task lists grouped into editable sections. Tap ⋯ next to a task → "reminder" to set a time; choose "repeat" for any interval.
- Script tab: scripts, hooks and captions per brand.
- Deals tab: brand deals, payments received and the monthly earnings goal.
- Calendar: tasks, batching days and payment deadlines by date.
- Menu (top right) → phone notifications, theme colour, account, delete account.
On first launch iOS asks to allow notifications. Reminders are scheduled on the device and arrive on the lock screen even when the app is closed. No sample files or extra setup are needed.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (supabase.com): user sign-in (email/password authentication) and database storage of each user's content (hosted in Canada).
- Netlify (netlify.com): hosts the app's web interface, which loads inside the app.
- Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and the Supabase code library.
- Apple frameworks: local notifications and haptics, on device.
The app uses no payment processors, AI services, analytics, advertising or tracking.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region where it is available.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app does not operate in a regulated industry and does not provide financial, medical or legal services. Payment tracking is a personal record that the user types in; the app does not move money. The only third-party material is two optional background music tracks from Pixabay ("Water Afro Pop Music" by Kontraa and "Chill Hop" by ZephiraMusic), used under the Pixabay Content License, which allows free commercial use.
