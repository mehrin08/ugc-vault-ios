UGC Vault Support

Need help with UGC Vault, found a bug, or have a feature idea? Email ugcrims@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

Do I need an account? Yes. A free account (email and password) keeps your lists, scripts, deals and payments synced across your phone and computer.

I forgot my password. On the log-in screen, tap "forgot password" and follow the email link.

How do I get reminders? On any task, tap the ⋯ button, then "reminder". To allow notifications on your iPhone, open the menu (top right), then "phone notifications".

How do I turn reminders off? Clear the reminder on the task, or turn off notifications in iPhone Settings → UGC Vault → Notifications.

How much does it cost? Try everything free for 3 days, then $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year (prices may vary by country).

How do I cancel or change my plan? In the app, open the menu, then subscription. Or on your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → UGC Vault. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.

I got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back? Sign in with the same Apple Account, open UGC Vault and tap "Restore purchases" on the subscribe screen.

Refunds? Apple handles billing, so request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account? Open the menu (top right), then account → delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of its data. See the Privacy Policy.

Is it on iPad or Android? Not as an app yet, but you can use it in any web browser. Email us if you'd like it.
